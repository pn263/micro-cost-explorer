The rule: MR = MC

  • If MR > MC, one more unit raises profit: produce more.
  • If MR < MC, one more unit lowers profit: produce less.
  • Profit is highest where MR = MC. With whole dozens: make every dozen whose MC is not above MR, and stop.
  • There the gap between TR and TC is the widest: the two curves have the same slope (slope of TR = MR, slope of TC = MC).

Produce or shut down? (this hour)

The decision rules

  • P ≥ ATC: profit (or zero). Produce where MR = MC and stay.
  • AVC ≤ P < ATC: a loss, but revenue pays the workers and materials (VC) and part of the rent (FC). Keep producing in the short run; exit in the long run.
  • P < AVC: revenue does not even pay VC. Shut down now: the loss is only FC.

Short run: shut down if P < min AVC (TR < VC). Long run: exit if P < min ATC (TR < TC). The rent already paid is a sunk cost: it is lost whether you open or not, so it should not drive the short-run choice.
