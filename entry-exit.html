Why profit is driven to zero

  • P > min ATC: profit → new factories enter → market supply shifts right → P falls.
  • P < min ATC: loss → factories exit → market supply shifts left → P rises.
  • It stops only at P = min ATC: zero economic profit, and every factory produces at its efficient scale (P = MC = min ATC).
  • With identical costs and free entry, the long-run supply curve is horizontal at min ATC.
  • Zero economic profit still pays the owner's opportunity cost: accounting profit is positive.
